Privacy Policy - Rental Manager RoomRent

Effective date: 12 October 2026

1. Introduction

Rental Manager RoomRent helps property owners and managers organise room information, tenant records, occupancy and rental payments. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

2. Information the App May Handle

Depending on the features you use, the app may process room details, tenant names and contact details, room assignments, rental amounts, payment entries, deposits, outstanding balances, and account information required for sign-in or cloud features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to support room management, tenant records, rent tracking, occupancy monitoring, and any backup or synchronisation features you choose to use.

4. Storage and Cloud Services

Information may be stored on your device. If cloud or account features are enabled, relevant information may be transmitted to and stored by the configured service providers, which may include Firebase. The actual storage and synchronisation depend on the app configuration.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate enabled app features or disclosed when required by law. The app operator should not sell personal information. Any additional sharing must be accurately described before this policy is published.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their devices and account credentials.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as needed for rental management, legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations. Users may contact the app operator using the email below to ask about access to or deletion of their information. Removing the app may not delete separate cloud records or backups.

8. Children's Privacy

Rental Manager RoomRent is intended for adults managing rental properties and is not designed for children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version should be available at the public URL linked from the Google Play listing.
